## C. The world and its places

### Q7. Names for the world, continents, realms and capitals.
The naming approach is recorded in `locations.md` (DRAFT): a consistent theme informed by real languages, starting with the human kingdoms. The names themselves are still open.
**Answer:** Leaning: no shared name for the world; each people names it in its own tongue. Naming themes by people, informed by real languages: still-blade's human kingdom Old English (fits Reeve, Moot, Elder); dwarves Old Norse; high elves Welsh-like; dark elves sharper, Latin-like; orcs short and hard; halflings plain rustic English. The names themselves wait for a naming session.

### Q8. The main present-day conflicts, per race.
**Answer:** Leaning, all regional: humans, rival kingdoms disputing the unclaimed wilds, beasts pressing out of them, tension over mages at home; dwarves, holding mountain passes, clan disputes over mines and runes; halflings, small farming peoples caught between larger neighbours, many on crown-granted land; high elves, an internal rift over war-magic, old enmity with dark elves; dark elves, intrigue between rival houses, old enmity with high elves; orcs, rival chieftains feuding for land and standing, some raiding borders, many selling their swords.

### Q9. Which races are allied, and which are not?
**Answer:** Leaning: no race-wide alliances; each kingdom, clan or house makes its own. Common pattern: humans, dwarves and halflings often allied through trade; elves tolerate humans at arm's length; high and dark elves are not allied; orcs make no formal alliances, but individual orcs hire out to anyone.

### Q10. The ancient escalations, and the great threat that could someday unite the races: what were or are they?
**Answer:** Leaning: the largest ancient escalations followed the moon's cracking, when the fall struck many lands and peoples fought over the fragments and the ruin. The future great threat stays undefined, with one hint: whatever cracked the moon may not be finished.

### Q11. What is "out there" beyond the RPG's training grounds? What threat does the Quartermaster mean?
**Answer:** Leaning: on the surface, simply the world beyond the gateway: the wilds, their beasts, rival kingdoms. He hints at more than he says, tied to his own mystery (Q63).

### Q12. Is "Alpha Island" a real place, and is the RPG's starting area really an island?
**Answer:** A build label, not a place. still-blade's starting area is a gateway into the threshold, not an island.

### Q13. Does "beautiful-tree" mean anything in the world (a working title, or a tree that matters)?
**Answer:** A working title only, with no meaning in the world.

## D. Peoples

### Q14. Which races lean Magic and which lean Martial?
On current cards, dwarves are mostly Martial and high and dark elves mostly Magic.
**Answer:** Leaning: Magic in the blood, strongest first: high elf, dark elf, orc and human (varies by line), halfling, dwarf. Martial by culture: dwarves, orcs and humans strongest. Dwarven runecraft is the Magic of their rare gifted; orcs honour their storm-callers, so stigma against mages is weakest among orcs and strongest among humans and dwarves; halflings have small folk magic (hedge witches).

### Q15. What are the races' in-world names for themselves?
**Answer:** Leaning: each race has its own name for itself in its own tongue; the games use human, dwarf and the rest as the translation, with native names as flavour. The names wait for the naming session.

### Q16. Are ogres and trolls peoples or beasts?
They're typed Beast, but "Hired Ogre" implies someone who takes pay.
**Answer:** Leaning: trolls are beasts (animal minds). Ogres speak a little, have no kingdom and hire out as muscle: a people in the world, not a playable race. The TCG keeps both typed Beast as a mechanic.

## E. Magic, Martial and specs

### Q17. Original in-world names for Magic, Martial, Command and charges.
**Answer:** Not yet. Keep Magic, Martial and Command as working names; part of the later naming session.

### Q18. Is blood magic a thing, and how does it fit Magic being drawn from the environment?
**Answer:** Leaning: it exists. Ordinary Magic draws on life already released into the world; blood magic draws on life still inside a body, one's own or another's. Costly, painful, and taboo among most peoples. Cinder Zealot fits.

### Q19. What is a "rift" (Rift Walk, Rift Mage, Riftweaver, Ysolde "the Unanchored")?
**Answer:** Leaning: a rift is a brief tear a mage opens into the threshold, used to strike or step from somewhere else. Rare, mostly a dark-elf art. Ysolde "the Unanchored" rifts so often she has come loose from any one place.

### Q20. Are spirits (Arcane Wisp, Spark Imp) a form of Magic, released life, or something else?
**Answer:** Leaning: released life that gathered thick enough to hold a shape before dispersing: wisps where much life lives or dies (old forests, battlefields). Imps are spirits a mage shaped with fire. Ties to Gathering.

### Q21. Are constructs magical?
Stone Sentinels are "conjured" and Rune Shards "rune-carved", but Brass Automaton and Siege Golem are Neutral constructs any Champion can field.
**Answer:** Leaning: two kinds. Binder constructs (Stone Sentinel, Rune Shard, golems) are moved by Magic bound into runes. Neutral constructs (Brass Automaton, Siege Golem, Siege Ballista) are machines built by engineers and need no gift, which is why any Champion can field them; "Siege Golem" is a soldier's nickname for a crewed war engine.

### Q22. How does Martial healing work in-world (Second Wind)?
**Answer:** Leaning: a fighter turns their whole inner reserve on their own body to drive it past a wound. It heals only oneself and costs everything at once (matches still-blade's Second Wind). Healing others needs surgeons, herbalists or Magic.

### Q23. Is there an in-world reason why Magic moves only enemies and Martial only its own units, why Casters pierce Armor, and why only Martial units Overrun?
**Answer:** The world rule is where the energy comes from: Magic is drawn from the world around the user, Martial from within. Neither is limited in how it acts (Martial could launch something outward). The TCG's movement, Armor and Overrun asymmetries are game design, not world rules.

### Q24. Where do the RPG's frost and healing spells fit?
Tempest covers fire and storm; no spec covers frost. Mend is self-healing magic. Do they belong to existing specs, future specs, or sub-specs?
**Answer:** Leaning: in the world, Magic can take any form (fire, frost, storm, healing). Which spec or path covers which is decided per game (see Q26).

### Q25. Are specs also organizations (an order, a guild)?
The plural names (Binders, Reavers) and the old deck names (The Arcane Circle, The Iron Company) suggest groups.
**Answer:** Leaning: specs are ways of fighting, not organizations. Local orders and guilds teach them and often give the joining quest; the Arcane Circle and the Iron Company are examples of such local groups. "Binders" and "Reavers" just mean practitioners.

### Q26. Are the four specs top-level paths, or sub-specs of something broader?
**Answer:** Paths, specs and their tiers are decided per game. World lore says only that cultures train fighters differently by region. still-blade may have paths only (undecided); the MMORPG gets more depth.

### Q27. Why do the dead come back in the RPG (player and enemy respawn)? An in-world reason, or purely a game mechanic?
**Answer:** A game mechanic only. Death in the world is real; no lore explains respawning.

### Q28. Is the RPG's level cap of 60 intended, given specs are earned around levels 5-10?
**Answer:** Not lore. Each game sets its own levels and cap.

## F. Characters

### Q29. Who are Champions (legends of history, present-day leaders, adventurers), and what does a TCG match represent?
**Answer:** Leaning: Champions are present-day figures (leaders, captains, renowned fighters), so they can appear in the RPG. A match is a battle one of them commands. Legendary units can be from the present or from history.

### Q30. What did Vaelith burn, and who exiled her? Is she connected to Ilvara, "the Last Flame" (also a high-elf fire mage)?
**Answer:** Leaning: Vaelith burned a living sacred grove to win a battle. High elves revere life as the source of Magic, so their ruling council exiled her. Ilvara, the Last Flame, was her teacher and the last of an old high-elf order of war-mages the council disbanded.

### Q31. Are Thoren Ashguard and Bram Ironvow, "the Unbreached" (both dwarf defenders), connected?
**Answer:** Leaning: Bram Ironvow is a dwarf of history whose gate was never breached; his vow is still sworn by dwarven pass-holders, Thoren among them.

### Q32. Is "The Unbroken Blade" a person or a weapon? What is Hroth's "Red Harvest"?
**Answer:** Leaning: both a weapon and a title: an old orc blade that has never broken, and the title of whoever wins and carries it. Hroth's "Red Harvest" is the battle in a harvest field where his sweeping axe held the field alone.

### Q33. Who will the Archmage and the Warlord become?
**Answer:** Leaning: the Archmage becomes a human Tempest mage and the Warlord an orc Reavers chieftain, giving humans and orcs their first Champions.

### Q34. Who does the RPG Quartermaster serve, and why is the player being trained? Is he connected to the dwarf Quartermaster card in the TCG?
**Answer:** Leaning: whom he serves and why he trains newcomers at the threshold stays his mystery (Q63). He is not the TCG's dwarf Quartermaster; that card is a role, not a person. still-blade will rename him to fit his mystic role (see Q64).

### Q42. Martial energy not restoring mid-fight (still-blade) vs TCG Champions gaining charges every turn and Bloodrush (Martial energy from kills in combat): a medium difference or a world rule? And why does it restore slowly in neutral zones like towns?
**Answer:** Leaning: Martial energy comes from within, so it doesn't seep back during a fight, but action kindles it: landed blows and kills (still-blade's charges and momentum, the TCG's Bloodrush). **Answer:** Leaning: rebirth is a literal new birth: a new character born with the gift who inherits something from the old one. No one gains Magic during a life. The system stays deferred.

**Answer:** Leaning: trainers are the "training" in "training, questing and practicing". A trainer teaches a technique once the student is ready; the level number is the game's measure of readiness.

### Q46. The sky-fallen upgrade materials: what fell, and when? Who is the artisan, and why can only they work the materials?
**Answer:** Leaning: the moon fragments fell across many lands, most heavily in still-blade's region, so most of what survives is there; elsewhere much was used up, decayed or buried over the millennia. Small amounts remain in other regions, where artisans work them in their own ways, so the materials behave differently by region. Still open: who the artisan is and why only they can work them in this region.

### Q47. Where do gems come from in the world? Are they sky-fallen like the upgrade materials, and why can the artisan work them?
**Answer:** Leaning: ordinary stones from the earth, not sky-fallen; monsters hoard bright things. Only moon-worked gear has sockets, and only the artisan can set a stone into moon-metal so it takes hold.
