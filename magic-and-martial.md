# Magic and Martial

The words "Magic", "Martial" and "Command" are working names. Original in-world names are wanted. PLACEHOLDER

## The two energies (CANON, TCG)
- **Magic is drawn from the environment.** Life generates it and releases it outward into the world, "like qi". A mage channels what the world around them produces.
- **Magic is born.** Without the innate gift, a person can never learn it.
- **The gift must still be trained.** A born mage needs training to use Magic properly. CANON
- **Martial energy comes from within**, "like jing". Anyone can develop it through discipline and training. At its height it is almost supernatural and comparable to Magic, but it is a different energy that behaves differently.
- **Aptitude is a spectrum on both sides.** Among those born with the Magic gift, some are strong and some are so weak they never unlock anything, and may not know they have it (perhaps only slightly better reflexes or a small gift). Martial can be learned by anyone with enough dedication, but some are naturally more gifted. Magic skills can be trained, but only by those born with the gift, so a Magic trainer is consistent with "born, never learned". DRAFT
- **Martial energy does not restore during combat** (TCG). Because it comes from within, a fighter restores it by sitting still to cultivate it, or slowly in neutral zones like towns. DRAFT (still-blade). It doesn't seep back during a fight, but **action kindles it**: landed blows and kills (still-blade's charges and momentum, the TCG's Bloodrush). TCG charges every turn abstract a whole army. It restores at rest because cultivating needs calm: in towns a fighter can lower their guard, in the wilds they can't. DRAFT
- **still-blade:** Magic and Martial restore the same way for now: a steady trickle, including during combat. DRAFT ("keep them both the same for now"). A per-game tuning choice; the world rule is the line above.
- **Both are arts beyond what untrained commoners can do.**
- **Where the energy comes from is the world rule:** Magic from the world around the user, Martial from within. Neither is limited in how it acts (Martial could launch something outward). CANON

## Prestige / rebirth (idea)
- A future prestige system (like a rebirth) might be how a character gains Magic. Deferred; still-blade offers all four paths at creation for now. DRAFT (idea)
- **Rebirth is a literal new birth:** a new character born with the gift, who inherits something from the old one. No one gains Magic during a life, so "Magic is born" holds. DRAFT

## Prejudice (CANON, TCG)
- Many see mages as an affront to nature, "taking what isn't theirs", while Martial power is seen as earned.
- This brings disdain and suspicion, **not open war**.
- The stigma is a viewpoint held by characters and groups, not a truth the games assert. "Factions" in the TCG rules is just a general term for groups (orders, guilds, churches and the like), not world-spanning factions. CANON Both sides have their own prejudices. Rules text never uses moralizing words like "greed", "drain" or "corrupt".
- Anti-magic flavor fits Martial roles like the Witch Hunter. Magic roles could include mages who hide, protect or heal. DRAFT

## Hybrids
- Holding both energies is possible but very rare. The energies oppose each other in the body, and it takes great discipline and specific circumstances. CANON (TCG)
- Idea: a Hybrid's energies pull against each other (spending one weakens or costs the other). DRAFT (TCG)
- **A Hybrid spec's prerequisites would be very demanding** (both energies, great discipline), which keeps Hybrids rare. In theory a Magic wielder could also harness Martial, but that would be too strong, so it stays very rare. DRAFT

## Mechanics with an in-world reason
All DRAFT unless marked. Source: TCG.
- **Command:** as a battle develops, the commander brings more forces to the front.
- **Growth:** a mage grows by channeling, a fighter by practice.
- **Deck limits by spec:** a Champion's deck holds only what their path has taught them. CANON
- **Gathering** (Binders): when a unit dies, its life is released into the world and mages can draw on it. Ties to "life generates Magic".
- **Stalwart** (Bulwark): a defender makes every blow cost the attacker; whoever strikes past the line at the commander is struck back.
- **Banking** (retired): "inner reserves", unused Martial charges carried over. Replaced by Stalwart.
- **Martial healing** exists "in its own ways" (Second Wind): a fighter turns their whole inner reserve on their own body to drive it past a wound. It heals only oneself and costs everything at once. Healing others needs surgeons, herbalists or Magic. DRAFT
  - In still-blade, Second Wind (Attacker path) needs and spends a completely full Martial energy bar to restore about a third of the fighter's health. DRAFT (game rule), matching the above.
- **Blood magic exists.** Ordinary Magic draws on life already released into the world; blood magic draws on life still inside a body, one's own or another's. Costly, painful, and taboo among most peoples: the one place the charge "taking what isn't theirs" is literally true. DRAFT. Cinder Zealot fits (a draft note: its Arrival hurts both Champions; not on the current card).

## Mechanics with flavor but no stated in-world reason
CANON as TCG rules. They are game design, not world rules: the world rule is only where each energy comes from (see above). CANON
- Magic units are **Casters** that channel **Spells**; Martial units perform **Techniques**. **Orders** are paid with Command only and usable by any Champion.
- **Movement pillar:** Martial moves its own units, Magic moves only enemy units (Displace), Neutral has no movement.
- Casters' combat damage ignores 1 Armor; only Martial units Overrun.
- Only Techniques can be played during combat.
- **Shield** (Champion-only absorbing pool) vs **Armor** (per-hit reduction on units).
- **Warded:** can't be targeted by enemy Spells.
- **Rift** words (Rift Walk, Rift Mage, Riftweaver): Magic attacking through any lane. Names PLACEHOLDER. A **rift** is a brief tear a mage opens into the threshold (see `locations.md`), used to strike or step from somewhere else. Rare, mostly a dark-elf art. Ysolde "the Unanchored" rifts so often she has come loose from any one place. DRAFT
- **Runes** (runesmiths, Rune Shards, Runestone Golems): a Binders and dwarf motif. DRAFT
- Champion levels 1-3; spec mechanics unlock at Level 2: Overchanneling, Gathering, Stalwart, Bloodrush. Names PLACEHOLDER.

## RPG prototype abilities (beautiful-tree)
Game content in the prototype, with no in-world explanation yet.
- **Fireball:** "Hurl a slow but heavy ball of flame." Fire, colored orange.
- **Frost Bolt:** "A fast shard of ice. Can be cast on the move." Frost, colored light blue.
- **Mend:** "Restore your own health. Cannot be cast while moving." Healing, colored green. Self-only; source of the power not stated.
- **Magic can take any form in the world** (fire, frost, storm, healing). Which spec or path covers which is decided per game. DRAFT
- **Strike:** "A quick melee blow." Melee, colored red-brown.
- **Auto Attack:** toggle melee swings.
- **The test character's mixed kit** (Strike plus spells on one Mana bar) is a test setup only, not a Hybrid. CANON
- **Power types: Mana, Energy, Rage, Focus.** Rage builds from swinging; Mana and Focus regenerate. Prototype borrowings from WoW-style class design only: the world has just Magic and Martial energy, and each game decides its own resource bars. DRAFT
- **Magic's resource might be "mana".** Floated in still-blade discussion (a Magic-path self-heal paid with mana); Magic's resource is still undecided. DRAFT (idea). Each game decides its resource bars (see above).
- **Death and respawn:** the player respawns at a spawn point; enemies respawn after a delay. A game mechanic only; death in the world is real. CANON
