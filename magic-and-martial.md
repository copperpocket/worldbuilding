# Magic and Martial

The words "Magic", "Martial" and "Command" are working names. Original in-world names are wanted. PLACEHOLDER

## The two energies (CANON, TCG)
- **Magic is drawn from the environment.** Life generates it and releases it outward into the world, "like qi". A mage channels what the world around them produces.
- **Magic is born.** Without the innate gift, a person can never learn it.
- **The gift must still be trained.** A born mage needs training to use Magic properly. CANON
- **Martial energy comes from within**, "like jing". Anyone can develop it through discipline and training. At its height it is almost supernatural and comparable to Magic, but it is a different energy that behaves differently.
- **Martial energy does not restore during combat.** Because it comes from within, a fighter restores it by sitting still to cultivate it, or slowly in neutral zones like towns. DRAFT (still-blade). Tension with TCG charges every turn and Bloodrush; no reason yet for slow restoring in towns. See open questions.
- **Both are arts beyond what untrained commoners can do.**

## Prestige / rebirth (idea)
- A future prestige system (like a rebirth) might be how a character gains Magic: reborn into a Magic path, or an ability awakening. Not an awakening of a latent innate gift; for now, think of it only as a prestige system. Deferred; still-blade offers all four paths at creation for now. DRAFT (idea)
- **Conflict:** gaining Magic this way clashes with "Magic is born, never learned". See open questions.

## Prejudice (CANON, TCG)
- Many see mages as an affront to nature, "taking what isn't theirs", while Martial power is seen as earned.
- This brings disdain and suspicion, **not open war**.
- The stigma is a viewpoint held by characters and groups, not a truth the games assert. "Factions" in the TCG rules is just a general term for groups (orders, guilds, churches and the like), not world-spanning factions. CANON Both sides have their own prejudices. Rules text never uses moralizing words like "greed", "drain" or "corrupt".
- Anti-magic flavor fits Martial roles like the Witch Hunter. Magic roles could include mages who hide, protect or heal. DRAFT

## Hybrids
- Holding both energies is possible but very rare. The energies oppose each other in the body, and it takes great discipline and specific circumstances. CANON (TCG)
- Idea: a Hybrid's energies pull against each other (spending one weakens or costs the other). DRAFT (TCG)

## Mechanics with an in-world reason
All DRAFT unless marked. Source: TCG.
- **Command:** as a battle develops, the commander brings more forces to the front.
- **Growth:** a mage grows by channeling, a fighter by practice.
- **Deck limits by spec:** a Champion's deck holds only what their path has taught them. CANON
- **Gathering** (Binders): when a unit dies, its life is released into the world and mages can draw on it. Ties to "life generates Magic".
- **Stalwart** (Bulwark): a defender makes every blow cost the attacker; whoever strikes past the line at the commander is struck back.
- **Banking** (retired): "inner reserves", unused Martial charges carried over. Replaced by Stalwart.
- **Martial healing** exists "in its own ways" (Second Wind). How is not said.
  - One possible how: in still-blade, Second Wind (Attacker path) needs and spends a completely full Martial energy bar to restore about a third of the fighter's health. DRAFT as a game rule; whether it explains Martial healing in the world isn't settled (see open questions, Q22).
- **Blood magic:** a draft note on Cinder Zealot (its Arrival hurts both Champions). Not on the current card.

## Mechanics with flavor but no stated in-world reason
CANON as TCG rules; the in-world reasons are open.
- Magic units are **Casters** that channel **Spells**; Martial units perform **Techniques**. **Orders** are paid with Command only and usable by any Champion.
- **Movement pillar:** Martial moves its own units, Magic moves only enemy units (Displace), Neutral has no movement.
- Casters' combat damage ignores 1 Armor; only Martial units Overrun.
- Only Techniques can be played during combat.
- **Shield** (Champion-only absorbing pool) vs **Armor** (per-hit reduction on units).
- **Warded:** can't be targeted by enemy Spells.
- **Rift** words (Rift Walk, Rift Mage, Riftweaver): Magic attacking through any lane. What a rift is in the world is undefined. PLACEHOLDER
- **Runes** (runesmiths, Rune Shards, Runestone Golems): a Binders and dwarf motif. DRAFT
- Champion levels 1-3; spec mechanics unlock at Level 2: Overchanneling, Gathering, Stalwart, Bloodrush. Names PLACEHOLDER.

## RPG prototype abilities (beautiful-tree)
Game content in the prototype, with no in-world explanation yet.
- **Fireball:** "Hurl a slow but heavy ball of flame." Fire, colored orange.
- **Frost Bolt:** "A fast shard of ice. Can be cast on the move." Frost, colored light blue.
- **Mend:** "Restore your own health. Cannot be cast while moving." Healing, colored green. Self-only; source of the power not stated.
- **Strike:** "A quick melee blow." Melee, colored red-brown.
- **Auto Attack:** toggle melee swings.
- **The test character's mixed kit** (Strike plus spells on one Mana bar) is a test setup only, not a Hybrid. CANON
- **Power types: Mana, Energy, Rage, Focus.** Rage builds from swinging; Mana and Focus regenerate. These come from WoW-style class design and may conflict with the no-classes vision. See open questions.
- **Magic's resource might be "mana".** Floated in still-blade discussion (a Magic-path self-heal paid with mana); Magic's resource is still undecided. DRAFT (idea). Tension: Magic is drawn from the environment, so an inner "mana" pool may not fit. See open questions (Q39).
- **Death and respawn:** the player respawns at a spawn point; enemies respawn after a delay. No in-world reason yet.
