# Peoples

## The races
- **Six starting races: human, dwarf, halfling, high elf, dark elf, orc.** CANON (TCG)
- **Others may come later** if the lore fits; undead is the example given. DRAFT (TCG)
- **Races should feel like real peoples, not generic units.** CANON (TCG)
- In-world names for the races: not yet defined. See open questions.

## Independence and politics
- **The races are fiercely independent.** Even allies only *tolerate* each other. Some are not allied at all and don't allow other races into their capitals unescorted. Which races are allied is not stated. CANON (TCG)
- **Each race governs itself.** CANON. The only examples of political form are "human kingdoms" and "orc chieftains". DRAFT (TCG)
- Each race has its own present-day conflicts; these are not yet defined.
- **The world is vast.** There are no world-spanning factions. One kingdom may know of another on the far side of the world only through rumor, and as strangers they are more likely hostile than allied. CANON

## Aptitude for Magic and Martial
- **Magic aptitude is partly in the blood:** some races are more likely to produce Magic users. CANON (TCG)
- **Any race can produce Martial fighters**, but culture and available knowledge make some races lean Martial. CANON (TCG)
- **Magic among humans:** some humans are born with the gift and some are not, possibly because of ancestry from races more likely to produce Magic users. This implies mixed ancestry between races. DRAFT (idea)
- Which races lean which way is **not stated**. On the current TCG cards, dwarves are mostly Martial and high and dark elves mostly Magic, but that is an observation, not a rule.

## How projects treat race
- **TCG:** race is background only (art, name style, a small note on the card), never part of a card's or Champion's name, and there are no race-synergy mechanics. CANON (TCG)
- **RPG:** the player selects from a list of available races at character creation (see `specializations.md`). CANON. The prototype currently has only human models (male in use, female in progress). DRAFT (RPG)
- **still-blade:** the player character is always human. A per-game rule, tied to a humans-only tournament (see `projects.md`). DRAFT
