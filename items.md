## Gear upgrading (still-blade)
- **Upgrading is a regional tradition that rests on one person.** In the human kingdom that holds the tournament, gear is made stronger by working in materials found mostly in that region (dropped by its monsters). A single old artisan created the technique and is the only one who can perform it; how or why they can harness the materials is a mystery. The kingdom protects and reveres them, and they serve its citizens, but only in that kingdom. This is why the kingdom's fighters keep one piece of gear and improve it rather than replacing it. DRAFT (the artisan's name, the materials and the mystery are undefined)
- **The materials are not of this world.** They are fragments of the cracked moon (see `history.md`). The fall struck many lands but most heavily this region, so most of what survives is here; elsewhere much was used up, decayed or buried over the millennia. Small amounts remain in other regions, where artisans work them in their own ways, so the materials behave differently by region. DRAFT. Who the artisan is and why only they can work them here is open (Q46). They are rare finds that glow on the ground and are announced nearby.
  - Three materials: **Skyiron** (raises level), **Starheart** (raises quality), **Shardstone** (raises plus, graded +1, +2...). Names PLACEHOLDER.
  - Rarely, working them opens sockets in the gear. DRAFT
- **Gems** can be set into sockets by the artisan; each raises a single stat: Strike Gem (damage), Guard Gem (defence), Vigor Gem (health), Swift Gem (speed). They drop rarely from monsters (mostly elites). Three gems of a grade combine into one of the next grade (I, II, III). A socketed gem can be removed for a fee instead of being lost. PLACEHOLDER (game mechanic). In the world they are ordinary stones from the earth, not sky-fallen; monsters hoard bright things. Only moon-worked gear has sockets, and only the artisan can set a stone into moon-metal so it takes hold. DRAFT
- **Rare named gear** exists alongside upgrading. Named items match ordinary gear in strength; they differ by a unique effect and look. PLACEHOLDER (no named items exist yet). Being regional, upgrading leaves room for WoW-style named loot elsewhere.

- **Metal coins:** copper, silver and gold (100 copper to a silver, 100 silver to a gold, written like "1g 20s 5c"). Humanoid monsters carry coin; beasts drop sellable parts instead (Pelt, Fang). PLACEHOLDER (game-system choice; no lore reason for either)
- **Second Wind Tonic:** a potion that refills the area skill's resource. PLACEHOLDER (reuses the name "Second Wind"; see `glossary.md`)
- **Training gear and gems:** Training Charm (a Magic ring), Training Band, Cap, Vest, Pendant and Boots; gems Strike, Guard, Vigor and Swift. PLACEHOLDER
- **Martial Power and Magic Power:** gear carries power of one energy, and only someone trained in that energy can draw on it. Martial gear does nothing for a Magic user's power, and the reverse. DRAFT (mechanic agreed; stat names PLACEHOLDER)
