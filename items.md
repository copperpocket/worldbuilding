## Gear upgrading (still-blade)
- **Upgrading is a regional tradition that rests on one person.** In the human kingdom that holds the tournament, gear is made stronger by working in materials found only in that region (dropped by its monsters). A single old artisan created the technique and is the only one who can perform it; how or why they can harness the materials is a mystery. The kingdom protects and reveres them, and they serve its citizens, but only in that kingdom. This is why the kingdom's fighters keep one piece of gear and improve it rather than replacing it. DRAFT (the artisan's name, the materials and the mystery are undefined)
- **The materials are not of this world.** They came from the sky, perhaps as a meteor or something similar; that is part of why they are found only in this region and why only the artisan can work them. DRAFT (idea; the source, the event and the link to the artisan's mystery are undefined). See open questions (Q46).
  - Three materials: **Skyiron** (raises level), **Starheart** (raises quality), **Shardstone** (raises plus, graded +1, +2...). Names PLACEHOLDER.
  - Rarely, working them opens sockets in the gear. DRAFT
- **Gems** can be set into sockets by the artisan; each raises a single stat: Strike Gem (damage), Guard Gem (defence), Vigor Gem (health), Swift Gem (speed). They drop rarely from monsters (mostly elites). Three gems of a grade combine into one of the next grade (I, II, III). A socketed gem can be removed for a fee instead of being lost. PLACEHOLDER (game mechanic). Their origin in the world is open; see open questions (Q47).
