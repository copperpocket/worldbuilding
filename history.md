# History

Almost no history is defined yet. The only dated event is the fall, below.

- **Wars between races have broken out many times.** CANON (TCG)
- **An ancient history of larger-scale escalations** is possible, "a story for another time". DRAFT (TCG). The largest followed the fall, when the fragments struck many lands and peoples fought over them and the ruin. DRAFT
- **A great threat that forces the races to band together** is possible later, but is not part of the present day. DRAFT (TCG). It stays undefined, with one hint: whatever cracked the moon may not be finished. DRAFT
- **Present day:** each race's main conflicts are in `peoples.md`.
- **Calendars:** each people counts years from its own most recent major event (a founding, a war, a crowning). DRAFT. Specifics open (Q66).
