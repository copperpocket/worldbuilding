# Creatures

All names PLACEHOLDER; none has lore beyond its name yet. The TCG code types non-people as Beast, Construct and Spirit.

## Beasts
- **TCG (all Neutral):** Carrion Crow, Hunting Hound, Marsh Adder, Den Mother and its Wolf Pup token, Pack Alpha, Ironhide Boar, Cave Bear, Hired Ogre, Ember Drake, Cave Troll, The Ashen Wyrm (Legendary).
- **Wolves are ordinary wildlife**, in the RPG (the Wolf, the first real enemy) and the TCG (Den Mother, Wolf Pup, Pack Alpha); no larger threat behind them. CANON
- **Mounts:** griffons exist as mounts (Griffon Rider, a human Martial unit).
- **Dragons / wyrms:** Ember Drake and The Ashen Wyrm.
- **Trolls are beasts** (animal minds). **Ogres** speak a little, have no kingdom and hire out as muscle: a people in the world, not a playable race. The TCG keeps both typed Beast as a mechanic. DRAFT

## Spirits
- Arcane Wisp (Neutral, Flying), Spark Imp (Tempest Caster).
- A Cinder spirit token existed in an early draft and was removed.
- **What spirits are:** released life that gathered thick enough to hold a shape before dispersing. Wisps form where much life lives or dies (old forests, battlefields); imps are spirits a mage shaped with fire. Ties to Gathering. DRAFT

## Constructs
- **Magic-made (Binders):** Stone Sentinel ("a conjured construct"), Rune Shard ("a rune-carved construct"), Runestone Golem, Rune Colossus, and Brunhal's "stone guardians". DRAFT
- **Mechanical or crafted (Neutral):** Brass Automaton, Siege Ballista, Siege Golem, Powder Keg, Wooden Barricade, and the Decoy token (Reavers).
- **Two kinds:** Binder constructs are moved by Magic bound into runes. Neutral constructs are machines built by engineers and need no gift, which is why any Champion can field them; "Siege Golem" is a soldier's nickname for a crewed war engine. DRAFT
