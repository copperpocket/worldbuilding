# Creatures

All names PLACEHOLDER; none has lore beyond its name yet. The TCG code types non-people as Beast, Construct and Spirit.

## Beasts
- **TCG (all Neutral):** Carrion Crow, Hunting Hound, Marsh Adder, Den Mother and its Wolf Pup token, Pack Alpha, Ironhide Boar, Cave Bear, Hired Ogre, Ember Drake, Cave Troll, The Ashen Wyrm (Legendary).
- **RPG:** **Wolf**, the first real enemy. An ordinary animal; no special or corrupted variant. DRAFT
- **Mounts:** griffons exist as mounts (Griffon Rider, a human Martial unit).
- **Dragons / wyrms:** Ember Drake and The Ashen Wyrm.
- **Ogres and trolls** appear only as Beasts (Hired Ogre, Cave Troll), though "Hired" implies someone who takes pay. See open questions.

## Spirits
- Arcane Wisp (Neutral, Flying), Spark Imp (Tempest Caster).
- A Cinder spirit token existed in an early draft and was removed.

## Constructs
- **Magic-made (Binders):** Stone Sentinel ("a conjured construct"), Rune Shard ("a rune-carved construct"), Runestone Golem, Rune Colossus, and Brunhal's "stone guardians". DRAFT
- **Mechanical or crafted (Neutral):** Brass Automaton, Siege Ballista, Siege Golem, Powder Keg, Wooden Barricade, and the Decoy token (Reavers).
