## Champions (TCG)
Champions are **named characters of the world**, each with a unique ability. CANON. Who they are (legends of history, present-day leaders, adventurers) is open. Champions are chosen by the player, not found in packs.

| Champion | Race | Spec | Concept | Ability (Level 3) | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Vaelith Arandor (she) | High elf | Tempest | "A war-mage exiled for what she burned." | Ember Lash: damage to a damaged enemy or the Champion | DRAFT |
| Brunhal Deepvein | Dwarf | Binders | "A runesmith who raises stone guardians." | Runic Binding: strengthen a construct, or a Rune Shard | DRAFT |
| Thoren Ashguard (he) | Dwarf | Bulwark | "A captain who has never lost a pass he was sent to hold." | Hold the Line: a unit guards; otherwise Shield | DRAFT |
| Sylra Vex | Dark elf | Reavers | "An assassin who leaves no witnesses." | Open the Wound: damage a damaged enemy | DRAFT |
| The Archmage | none | Tempest | Prototype placeholder; to be named and reworked | Arcane Study: draw a card | PLACEHOLDER |
| The Warlord | none | Reavers | Prototype placeholder; to be named and reworked | Battle Cry: your units get +1 attack | PLACEHOLDER |

Notes:
- Vaelith's "exiled" implies a society that exiled her; who, and what she burned, is not stated.
- Brunhal's "stone guardians" match the Binders' Stone Sentinel and Rune Shard constructs.
- The Archmage and the Warlord are kept as second Champions of their specs.

## RPG characters (beautiful-tree)
- **Quartermaster:** the only NPC and quest giver. A job title, not a personal name. Dry, gruff, drill-sergeant tone: "Those dummies won't break themselves." / "Not bad. You might survive out there after all." The title implies a garrison, army or camp. DRAFT
- **The Player:** human in the prototype (male model in use, female in progress), level 1 to a cap of 60. No name or origin lore. DRAFT. In the full RPG the player picks from the available races (CANON); progression is in `specializations.md`.

## still-blade characters
- **The artisan:** an unnamed old artisan in the human kingdom that holds the tournament. The only one who can upgrade gear with the region's sky-fallen materials, a technique they created; how is a mystery. Protected and revered by the kingdom; serves only its citizens. DRAFT. See `items.md`.

## Roles and archetypes
Card names describe roles, not individuals. The role idea is CANON ("names are unique roles or characters, e.g. Grunt, Inquisitor"); the specific names are PLACEHOLDER. From the TCG card list:

- **Commoners and hirelings (Neutral):** Militia Spearman, Pathfinder, Lookout, Border Scout, Camp Surgeon, Lamplighter, Veiled Informant (dark elf), Veteran Sellsword, Tollgate Keeper (dwarf), Slinger, Bounty Hunter (orc), Wandering Friar, Herbalist, Caravan Guard, Mercenary Captain, Quartermaster (dwarf), Lorekeeper (high elf), Militia Captain.
- **Magic practitioners (Casters):** Apprentice Pyromancer, Hedge Witch (halfling), Ward Apprentice, Battlemage Initiate, Spirit Mender, Rift Skirmisher, Rift Mage, Riftweaver (all three dark elf), Storm Adept, Elder Magus, Sky Sage. Binders: Stonewright (dwarf), Thornbinder and Archivist of Echoes (dark elf). Tempest: Cinder Zealot, Storm Herald, Stormcaller (all orc).
- **Martial fighters:** Longbow Archer, Drill Sergeant, Duelist, Mountain Axeman (dwarf), Outrider, Witch Hunter, Blademaster, Griffon Rider, Warmaster. Bulwark: Phalanx Veteran, Shieldbearer, Oathsworn Warden, Shieldwall Captain, Ironclad Knight. Reavers: Gap Runner, Venomblade, Bloodrager, Raider Chief.
- **Witch Hunter:** a Martial role with anti-magic flavor that fits the prejudice against mages. DRAFT
- **Inquisitors:** Magic, leaning. They wield Magic, so they are born with the gift; the name implies spell wielders. If their role is anti-magic, they may still use spells to do it. DRAFT. No Inquisitor card exists yet.
