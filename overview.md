# Overview

The essentials of the world. Read this first; open other files only when the task needs them.

The world, its continents and its realms have no names yet.

## Tone
- **Dark fantasy, kept grounded.** Conflicts are regional, never universal or planetary. CANON
- **No "faction vs faction"** (no WoW-style Alliance and Horde). Each race governs itself, like countries in real life. CANON
- **Wars between races have broken out many times in history**, and each race has its own conflicts. CANON
- A great threat that forces the races to band together is possible someday, but is **not** part of the present day. DRAFT

## Core theme: Magic vs Martial (CANON)
- **Neither is stronger, and neither is the villain.** Magic must feel as heroic and attractive as Martial. Never make Magic strictly stronger than Martial.
- **Magic** is drawn from the environment: life generates it, and a mage channels what the world produces. It is born, never learned, though the gift must be trained.
- **Martial** energy comes from within and is cultivated through discipline and training. Anyone can develop it. At its height it is almost supernatural.
- Many see mages as an affront to nature ("taking what isn't theirs"), which brings disdain and suspicion, not open war. **This stigma is a viewpoint held by characters, not a truth the games assert**, and both sides have prejudices. Rules text never moralizes.
- Holding both energies (a **Hybrid**) is possible but very rare.

Details: `magic-and-martial.md`.

## Peoples (CANON)
Six starting races: **human, dwarf, halfling, high elf, dark elf, orc.** They are fiercely independent; even allies only tolerate each other. They should feel like real peoples, not generic units. Details: `peoples.md`.

## Characters and paths (CANON, general vision)
In the RPG a character picks a race, not a class, trains for their first levels, then earns a **combat specialization** through a quest. Details: `specializations.md`.
