# Combat Specializations ("specs")

"Spec" is the working word; an in-world name can come later. All spec names are PLACEHOLDER. Specs are paths a character joins, not peoples or nations.

## How a spec is earned (CANON, general vision, TCG)
- In the RPG, a character picks a **race, not a class**, at creation.
- Over their first levels (about 5-10) they build skills by training, questing and practicing.
- They are then offered a **quest to join a combat specialization**, which needs skill points and other prerequisites.
- Leaving one spec for another "might be possible". DRAFT
- In the TCG, a Champion's deck holds only what their path has taught them.

### RPG progression model (DRAFT, leaning; prototyping only)
- At creation the player picks only a race and customizations, no class.
- In game they skill up and are eventually given the opportunity to become a **"class"** (working word): their combat class, the most basic thing that determines their combat abilities.
- They have some choice: pursue the class that best fits their skills, or work on other skills and try for a different class opportunity.
- Afterwards, something similar determines their **spec**, and maybe a third tier.
- **Conflict:** the CANON above has a single tier (a quest to join a spec). Under this model the four specs could be classes or specs; see open questions (Q26).

The prototype's WoW-style power types (Mana/Energy/Rage/Focus) are still unresolved; see open questions.

## Regional differences (DRAFT, still-blade)
- The world isn't fully discovered, and cultures train fighters differently by region.
- In the (unnamed) region where still-blade is set, people are trained from youth into one of four broad paths: **Attacker, Defender, Ranged** (Martial) and **Magic** (only the born-gifted). The Magic path chooses a specialization later, around a high level. Path names PLACEHOLDER.
- Elsewhere, specs are earned through a quest as described above.
- Open: whether Martial paths also pick a spec later, how the paths relate to the four specs, and how the Magic path's spec is earned. See open questions.
- **Skills come from weapons and from paths** (still-blade). Each weapon gives its own skill shot: Line Strike (blade), Wide Sweep (polearm), Piercing Shot (bow). Each path unlocks its own skills by level (up to 3): Second Wind (Attacker), Guard (Defender), Fan Shot (Ranged). PLACEHOLDER (names and numbers are game tuning). Differs from "skills built by training, questing and practicing" above; see open questions (Q45).
  - Second Wind spends a full Martial energy bar to restore about a third of health. DRAFT (game rule). See `magic-and-martial.md`.

## The four specs (DRAFT concepts, PLACEHOLDER names)

| Spec | Energy | Identity |
|---|---|---|
| **Tempest** | Magic | Destruction: burn, area damage, damage to the enemy Champion. Fragile, expensive bodies |
| **Binders** | Magic | Summoning and control: constructs, Root, slowing the enemy |
| **Bulwark** | Martial | Defense: Armor, Shield, an unbreakable front line. Wins by making attacks costly |
| **Reavers** | Martial | Aggression: swift units, finishing damaged enemies, Venom. Low health |

## Other paths
- **Hybrid specs** (both energies) are planned; none exists yet. They are very rare. CANON that they exist, DRAFT for any specific one.
- **Sub-specs** (like WoW's Mage -> Frost Mage) are an open idea; the four specs may turn out to be sub-specs. DRAFT
- **A Neutral Champion**, "a leader of commoners, beasts and constructs", is a future idea. DRAFT
