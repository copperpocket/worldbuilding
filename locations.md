# Locations

**No named location exists yet.** The world, continents, realms and capitals are all unnamed.

**Naming approach:** names should follow a consistent theme (world, continents, realms, capitals, kingdoms), chosen with help and informed by real languages. Start with the human kingdoms. An ongoing discussion. DRAFT

## In the world
- **Capitals** of the races exist; some are closed to other races unless escorted. CANON (TCG)
- **The battlefield** (TCG lore basis): two lines facing each other across a field split into lanes; the Champion commands from behind their line, open to any enemy who finds a lane with nobody in the way. CANON as the TCG's framing. (An earlier version had Vanguard and Rear rows; retired.)
- **A tournament in the human kingdom:** only humans may take part, and only humans are allowed there. What it is and who holds it is undefined. DRAFT (still-blade). That kingdom's region is the only source of the sky-fallen gear-upgrade materials, dropped by its monsters, and home to the one artisan who can work them. DRAFT (still-blade; see `items.md`)
- **A pass** that Thoren Ashguard was sent to hold. DRAFT (TCG)
- **"Out there":** the RPG Quartermaster refers to somewhere dangerous beyond the training area. DRAFT, implied only (RPG)
- **Alpha Island:** the RPG prototype's only real zone, with the Quartermaster, training dummies and wolves. Likely a build label ("alpha"), not a place name. PLACEHOLDER (RPG)
- **Terrain words in TCG card names only:** marsh, caves, mountains, borders, tollgates, caravans, camps. PLACEHOLDER
