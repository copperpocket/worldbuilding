# Locations

**No named location exists yet.** The world, continents, realms and capitals are all unnamed.

**Naming approach:** names should follow a consistent theme (world, continents, realms, capitals, kingdoms), chosen with help and informed by real languages. Start with the human kingdoms. An ongoing discussion. DRAFT

## In the world
- **Capitals** of the races exist; some are closed to other races unless escorted. CANON (TCG)
- **The battlefield** (TCG lore basis): two lines facing each other across a field split into lanes; the Champion commands from behind their line, open to any enemy who finds a lane with nobody in the way. CANON as the TCG's framing. (An earlier version had Vanguard and Rear rows; retired.)
- **A tournament in the human kingdom:** only humans may take part, and only humans are allowed there. What it is and who holds it is undefined. DRAFT (still-blade). That kingdom's region is the only source of the sky-fallen gear-upgrade materials, dropped by its monsters, and home to the one artisan who can work them. DRAFT (still-blade; see `items.md`)
- **A pass** that Thoren Ashguard was sent to hold. DRAFT (TCG)
- **"Out there":** the RPG Quartermaster refers to somewhere dangerous beyond the training area. DRAFT, implied only (RPG)
- **Alpha Island:** the RPG prototype's only real zone, with the Quartermaster, training dummies and wolves. Likely a build label ("alpha"), not a place name. PLACEHOLDER (RPG)
- **Terrain words in TCG card names only:** marsh, caves, mountains, borders, tollgates, caravans, camps. PLACEHOLDER

## Outside the world

## The planet (worldbuilding, 2026-11-15)
- **An Earth-like planet in its own universe,** not our Earth in another age. CANON
- **Larger than an average Earth-like planet,** which explains the great distances between kingdoms. CANON
- **Seasons exist** in the lore. Each game decides whether to show them. CANON
- **Several large continents, not one supercontinent.** CANON. Five or six. DRAFT (count, sizes and shapes undecided; see open questions)
- **No kingdom rules a whole continent,** barring a rare exception, because of the size and the resources it would take. CANON
- **The peoples have no complete map.** Kingdoms on different continents may not know each other exist. still-blade's continent knows several other continents through sea trade. DRAFT
- **The moon is cracked,** and its fragments are the sky-fallen materials. What cracked it is a mystery to the inhabitants and to the author: a natural cosmic event or something mystical. See `history.md`. DRAFT
- **Creation:** unexplained for now. The inhabitants wonder where they came from and hold their own creation myths, which need not be correct, and one must exist in-world before a game ships. The author's own answer may grow as the world is built. Magic and Martial energy suggest some cosmic or higher power; hint at it early. DRAFT

## still-blade's slice of the world (DRAFT)
- **Continent:** mid-sized, with a coastal kingdom that covers only part of it. The kingdom is one of several human kingdoms.
- **Layout:** a mountain range along one side shelters the kingdom's heartland. A great river runs from the range to the sea, and the capital sits near its mouth or on its plain, in temperate land. Unclaimed wilds lie inland.
- **The capital's province** is coastal and temperate and has a Duke or Duchess, whose seat is a second city. The intro town and the capital are in it, close together. See `human-kingdoms.md`.
- **A nearby province** is cool, misty and maritime, with moors, marsh and forest inland. Players travel to it later to keep levelling.
- **The intro town** is inland, smaller than a seat, and is the first settlement after the intro. It holds the Guide, a market of several vendors and trainers. The player quests around it from about level 3 to 10, meeting hamlets and villages; the capital is introduced at about level 10, which may stretch to 20 or beyond. The capital is on the game map.
- **The game's playable world** is the capital's province, the nearby province and the wilds beyond, not a whole continent.
- Names, the seat city and the exact positions are open.

## The threshold (DRAFT)
- **The intro garrison is a threshold:** a magical place outside normal space, entered once, with a door that opens one way, so a character can never return. It is the instanced intro zone of still-blade, covering about levels 1 and 2.
- **The Quartermaster keeps it,** alone, meeting newcomers and training them gruffly. He has a deeper story nobody knows yet (see `characters.md`).
- **It may connect** to the world's creation and mystic story, to portals and magical items, and to other entities that live in it. Nothing is defined.
- Player characters are **not** a defined special kind of person in the lore. CANON
